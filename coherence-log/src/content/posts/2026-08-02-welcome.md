This is the first entry in Coherence Log — a site that tracks two things I follow closely: quantum sensing research (spin defects, ODMR, photonic integration) and the tools and trends shaping day-to-day software engineering.

Most new posts here will be drafted automatically every ten days from recent papers, preprints, and engineering write-ups, then reviewed before publishing. The goal isn't to compete with news aggregators — it's to add a working researcher's read on what's actually worth paying attention to, and why.

Two tracks, one log: entries tagged **quantum** cover sensing, defect qubits, and photonics; entries tagged **software** cover tooling, languages, and infrastructure trends relevant to scientific and general software work.
